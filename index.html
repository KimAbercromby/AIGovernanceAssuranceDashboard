How to read success

Is the process running?

  • Register coverage and owners assigned
  • Reviews on time, DPIAs before go-live
  • Approval time: governance should be fast, not a blocker

Is it preventing harm?

  • Breaches and serious complaints falling
  • Incidents closed within the service standard
  • Actions from findings completed on time

Traps to avoid

  • Zero overrides is a warning, not success: staff may be rubber-stamping the AI
  • Fewer shadow AI finds only counts once detection is mature
  • A green dashboard with no actions means nobody is looking
